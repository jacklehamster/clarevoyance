Village — Talk & Collect

A small quest, start to finish. Talk to the villagers in an old-school dialogue box — the speaker's face, text typed out, the odd choice — where what they say depends on what you chose and what you carry. Gather moonherbs and coins, earn a key, buy a lantern, get past the guard. SPACE to talk.

 🗺️

Explore — Top-Down

A world to walk around in, seen from above at 45°: ruins, roads, a village and a pond set in endless procedural wilds — every plant grows from its coordinates, so it is always there when you come back. WASD to walk, Q/E to turn the camera, V to switch view.

 🧱

Explore — Dungeon Crawl

The same world from behind the hero: a third-person dungeon crawler with walls, sky, and a camera that turns in 90° steps. Head north through the gate into the ruins. WASD to walk, Q/E to turn, V to switch view.

 🗝️

Quest Demo

Find 3 keys, open the counter-gated door, dodge a patrolling enemy previewed by a real 1-second-ahead shim ghost, and reach the win room. Bitmap-font text, archetypes, flags, and set_motion, all in one scene.

World Demo

A tiled room: pitched floor quads, brick walls from a second sprite sheet, and a translucent ghost penguin — the shim render path.

Controls Demo

Menu Demo
